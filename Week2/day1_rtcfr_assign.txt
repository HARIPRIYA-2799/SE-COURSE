### 🥗 Your "complete meal" formula

Rather than trying to get **all carbs, fibre and protein in one meal**, aim to balance your **whole day**:

**Each main meal:**

* 🥦 **½ plate:** vegetables
* 🍚 **¼ plate:** carbs — rice, millet, idli, dosa, chapati, etc.
* 🫘 **¼ plate:** protein — dal, sambar, kootu, chana, rajma, sprouts, tofu, paneer
* 🥛 Add **curd/buttermilk** where appropriate
* 🥜 Small amount of nuts/seeds for healthy fats

For a vegetarian trying to lose weight, **protein is particularly important** because it helps preserve muscle while you're losing weight.

Also, don't eliminate rice or other carbohydrates. **Portion control + adequate protein + vegetables + regular activity** is generally more sustainable than a very low-carb diet.
